Initial Thoughts
Question 1: Could an online presence grow the business by up to 50%?
Answer: Yes.

Reasoning: Currently, 90% of sales are local foot traffic. By implementing an e-commerce platform, the "addressable market" shifts from a single suburb to the entire country. High-quality, locally sourced pet food is a massive trend (humanisation of pets).
Risk: Growth of 50% requires a complete overhaul of Harry’s warehouse management and shipping logistics.

Question 2: Could changing to an international supply chain reduce costs by up to 24%?
Answer: Maybe (but proceed with caution).

Reasoning: Bulk buying ingredients from international markets usually lowers "cost per unit." However, the business’s USP (Unique Selling Point) is quality and local sourcing.  
Risk: Switching to international suppliers could damage the brand reputation and introduce supply chain "shocks" (shipping delays, customs), which negates the "10-minute drive" security they currently have.

Question 3: Could the business lose 33% of its existing customers without online features?
Answer: Yes.

Reasoning: Consumer habits are shifting. If a local competitor offers "click and collect" or a subscription model for heavy bags of dog food, Pampered Pets' customers will prioritise convenience.
Risk: Loyalty only goes so far; if the friction of buying (driving, parking, manual emailing) is too high, customers will churn.

Proposed Digitalisation Changes

E-commerce Portal: Moving from manual emails to a Shopify/WooCommerce site.

Inventory Management System (IMS): Replacing Harry’s spreadsheet with a cloud-based system that updates in real-time.

CRM (Customer Relationship Management): To track pet birthdays and send automated marketing, boosting loyalty.

However, risks aren't limited to digital tools…the biggest risk is typically the people operating the tools. The employees will need training, which costs time and money, and the biggest risk in any digitalisation is user resistance and lack of technical skill.

Sources
1. Market Growth & Trends (Backing the 50% Growth)
These sources provide the data needed to justify why an online presence could significantly increase revenue.

UK Pet Food Market Report 2025 (Mintel): This report highlights that "value growth will be driven by trading up" and identifies a massive trend toward "homemade" and "natural" pet foods…directly matching Pampered Pets' USP. [Source: Mintel Store]
UK Pet Supplies E-Commerce Market 2024-2029 (ECDB): Statistics show that the UK pet e-commerce market reached an online share of 15-20% in 2024. For a business currently at 0% online sales, capturing even a fraction of this national market supports the 50% growth theory. [Source: ECDB]
European Pet Food Facts and Figures 2025 (FEDIAF): Confirms that 49% of European households own a pet and that the market grew by 9% in a single year (2023-2024), indicating a "blooming" industry ready for digital expansion. [Source: PET Worldwide]

2. Supply Chain Analysis (International vs. Local)
We can use these to argue the "Maybe/No" regarding switching to international suppliers for a 24% cost reduction.

7 Key Benefits of Short Food Supply Chains (SpendEdge): This source details how "Short Food Supply Chains" (like Pampered Pets' 10-minute drive) reduce risk, increase quality, and improve consumer trust…factors often lost when moving to international sourcing. [Source: SpendEdge]
Global Pet Food Volatility & Resilience 2026 (Fastmarkets): Explains that while international sourcing seems cheaper, "localised events can instantly become global pricing crises" due to tariffs and climate impacts on crops, potentially wiping out that 24% cost saving. [Source: Fastmarkets]
Sustainable Development in SFSC (MDPI): A critical literature review showing that short supply chains are a "necessary condition" for high-quality food production, which is the core of Pampered Pets' reputation. [Source: MDPI]

3. Cybersecurity & Risk Management (Methodology)
These are the "Gold Standard" sources for your Risk Assessment methodology.

NCSC Small Business Guide (UK National Cyber Security Centre): The essential guide for small shops. It provides a 5-step framework: Backing up data, Malware protection, Keeping smartphones safe, Password security, and Avoiding phishing. [Source: NCSC/NBCC]
NCSC Annual Review 2024/2025: Provides the "Threat Landscape" data your report needs, specifically highlighting how "supply chain attacks" and "vulnerabilities in smaller suppliers" are rising. [Source: ISMS.online]
SANS Institute: Network Security for SMBs: A whitepaper specifically for Small and Mid-sized Businesses (SMBs) that helps prioritise threats when transitioning from a manual to a digital environment. [Source: SANS Institute]

4. Consumer Behavior & Digital Features (Backing the 33% Loss)
These sources back the idea that customers will leave if the shop doesn't modernise.

8 Pet Industry Trends for 2025 (Multidev): Reports that 70% of shoppers now demand "omnichannel" features, such as the ability to buy online and pick up in-store (Click & Collect). Without this, Pampered Pets is at risk of losing a third of its base. [Source: Multidev]
Digital Pet Landscape UK (GlobalPETS): Reveals that the fastest-growing categories are subscription-based pet foods. If Pampered Pets doesn't offer a digital subscription for their premium food, customers may switch to brands that do. [Source: GlobalPETS]
Loyalty in Pet Retail (DMN): Explains that traditional pet retailers lose customers because their loyalty programs lack the "personalisation or convenience" of digital-first competitors. [Source: DMN.ca]
